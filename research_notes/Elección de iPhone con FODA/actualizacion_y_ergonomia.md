## 1. Weight and thickness, all relevant models

### Takeaway
The iPhone Air (165 g, 5.64 mm) is still by far the lightest and thinnest iPhone, and Apple has no new light model this cycle. The 2026 flagships went heavier:
- iPhone 18 Pro: 211 g, +5 g vs the 17 Pro.
- iPhone 18 Pro Max: 249 g, +16 g. Heaviest non-folding iPhone ever.
- iPhone Duo: 254 g. Heaviest iPhone overall.

For a lightness-first buyer, moving from an iPhone 16 or 17 to any 18 Pro model is a step backwards in weight. Only the Air is a step forward.

## 2. Battery life from standardized independent tests

### Takeaway
- **Longest-lasting current iPhone:** the 18 Pro Max, which tops Tom's Guide, GSMArena and a 32-phone comparison.
- **Shortest:** the **iPhone Air without its battery pack**, with the lowest Tom's Guide web-surfing result of the lineup (12:02). That is about 45 minutes behind the iPhone 17, and the 17e and iPhone 16 sit in the same ~12.5–12.8 h band.
- **Air with the MagSafe Battery:** reaches 17:15, near Pro Max territory, but it adds weight and bulk.
- **18 Pro vs 17 Pro:** about one hour more in Tom's Guide's test (16:17 vs 15:21).

## 4. Concrete improvements per upgrade path, and iOS 27 / Apple Intelligence hardware gating

### Takeaway
The 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max bring a 2 nm A20 Pro chip, a variable-aperture main camera, Pro manual controls, Apple's C2 modem, a smaller Dynamic Island, faster charging and better cooling. Reviewers say 17 Pro owners can skip them.

The meaningful software gate in iOS 27 is **RAM**. Two top-tier Siri AI features need 12 GB:
- **Have 12 GB:** iPhone Air, 17 Pro, 17 Pro Max. The 18 Pro, 18 Pro Max and Duo very likely do too.
- **Have 8 GB and miss the two features:** iPhone 16, iPhone 17 and 17e.

### Cited Findings

**iPhone 18 Pro vs 17 Pro**

| Area | What changed |
|---|---|
| Main camera | Variable aperture f/1.48 / f/1.8 / f/2.8 / f/4.0 (the 17 Pro is fixed at f/1.78). |
| Pro controls (18 Pro only) | Manual aperture, shutter and white balance, also in video. |
| Cinematic mode | 4K at 60 fps (the 17 Pro does 4K/30). |
| Chip | 2 nm A20 Pro, Apple claims +40% sustained performance. |
| Modem | Apple C2 replaces Qualcomm, keeps US mmWave. |
| Display | Smaller Dynamic Island showing 3 Live Activities (2 on the 17 Pro). No reported panel change. |
| Charging | 50% in about 15 min. |
