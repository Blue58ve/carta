## 2. iPhone Air (1st generation): US fact sheet

### Takeaway
At 165 g and 5.64 mm, the Air is the lightest and thinnest iPhone in the lineup (the Duo is 5.2 mm only when unfolded). It now costs **$1,099 (256 GB) / $1,299 (512 GB) / $1,699 (1 TB)** and starts at 256 GB. Trade-offs: a single 48 MP rear camera, a small 3,149 mAh battery (about 12 h in Tom's Guide's web test vs. about 18 h for a 17 Pro Max) and weak, tinny audio.

- Moving from an iPhone 16 or 17, the buyer loses the ultra-wide camera (both have a 48 MP main plus ultra-wide). Battery life will likely be similar to or slightly below an iPhone 17 (12:02 vs 12:47 in Tom's Guide's test). This matters for casual photos (no wide group or landscape shots, no macro via ultra-wide).

## 3. iPhone Duo (Apple's foldable): US fact sheet

### Takeaway
The iPhone Duo is real and orderable: **$1,999 (256 GB) / $2,199 (512 GB) / $2,599 (1 TB) / $3,199 (2 TB)**. At **254 g** it is **Apple's heaviest iPhone ever**, 5 g heavier than the 249 g iPhone 18 Pro Max. It measures **11.3 mm folded and 5.2 mm unfolded**, with a **5.4-inch outer and 7.6-inch inner** display, an A20 Pro chip, a 4,883 mAh battery and Touch ID (no Face ID). Reviewers praise its battery life and near-invisible crease. Their criticisms are the weight, a cramped outer screen and many apps still letterboxed on the inner screen. It is **sold out until late January 2027**.

## 4. SIM: eSIM-only?

### Takeaway
Both models are **eSIM-only in the US**. The iPhone Air is not compatible with physical SIM cards. The iPhone Duo is reported as eSIM-only in every market, so no physical-SIM version exists anywhere.

### Cited Findings
- iPhone Air: Apple's spec page says it uses eSIM "not compatible with physical SIM cards". It supports dual eSIM (two active) and stores 8 or more eSIMs — [Apple Support tech specs](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125092); [Apple Support (QA)](https://support.apple.com/en-qa/125092)
- iPhone Duo: "only available with eSIM, regardless of where you buy one" — [Tom's Guide Duo vs 18 Pro Max](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/weve-tested-the-iphone-duo-and-iphone-18-pro-max-heres-the-phone-you-should-actually-buy); [AppleInsider comparison](https://appleinsider.com/inside/iphone-18/vs/iphone-18-pro-vs-iphone-duo-apples-new-models-compared)
- For context, the iPhone 18 Pro Max is eSIM-only in North America but has physical-SIM versions in other regions — [Ghostek](https://ghostek.com/blogs/articles/apple-iphone-duo-vs-iphone-18-pro-max); [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/weve-tested-the-iphone-duo-and-iphone-18-pro-max-heres-the-phone-you-should-actually-buy)

### Inferences
- Every US iPhone 17 and 16 is already eSIM-only, so this is not a new constraint for a US buyer coming from an iPhone 16/17. It matters only if the buyer travels to countries where local carriers are not eSIM-friendly.

### Gaps
- No source was retrieved confirming whether any regional Air variant (e.g., China) uses a physical SIM. Apple's wording ("not compatible with physical SIM cards") suggests eSIM-only worldwide.

## 5. What reviewers say: comfort, one-hand use, battery anxiety, camera limits, common complaints

### Takeaway
**Air:** nearly everyone praises how light and thin it feels in the hand. Common complaints are a single camera, average battery life (Pro models last much longer), weak audio and a price that is high for what you get. Verdicts range from "best iPhone ever" to "least sensible iPhone ever". **Duo:** strong battery life and a crease that is hard to see. Complaints are the 254 g weight (tiring one-handed), a cramped outer screen and, at launch, many apps still letterboxed on the inner screen.

### Cited Findings
**iPhone Air**
- TechRadar: "the sexiest iPhone I've ever tested". It is not the best iPhone for photography or endurance, "but as a design statement, it's a gem" — [TechRadar review](https://www.techradar.com/phones/iphone/apple-iphone-air-review)
- PhoneArena headline: "Pointless until you actually hold it in your hand" — [PhoneArena review](https://www.phonearena.com/reviews/apple-iphone-air-review_id7688)
- Tech Advisor: "The least sensible iPhone ever". Too much was sacrificed for the slim design — [Tech Advisor](https://www.techadvisor.com/article/2945442/apple-iphone-air-review.html)
- Macworld: "A whole lot less for a whole lot more". 3.5/5, with tinny, thin audio — [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/2926985/iphone-air-review-a-whole-lot-less-for-a-whole-lot-more.html)
- GSMArena: "the actual in-hand feeling of the Air left us puzzled", though it feels slimmer than the Galaxy S25 Edge thanks to curved edges and corners — [GSMArena review](https://www.gsmarena.com/apple_iphone_air-review-2885p2.php)

## 6. MagSafe battery pack for the Air

### Takeaway
Apple sells a dedicated **iPhone Air MagSafe Battery ($99, 3,149 mAh)**. It raises Apple's video claim from 27 h to 40 h and lifted Tom's Guide's web test from 12:02 to 17:15. Drawbacks: slow charging (about 12 W), it fits only the Air, and Engadget advises against buying it. Its weight was not confirmed in this research.

### Cited Findings
- $99, 3,149 mAh (12.26 Wh), the same capacity as the phone's own battery. It charges at up to about 12 W wireless in most cases, while third-party Qi2 packs reach 15 W on the Air. Engadget's headline: "You absolutely should not buy Apple's iPhone Air MagSafe battery pack" — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/mobile/smartphones/you-absolutely-should-not-buy-apples-iphone-air-magsafe-battery-pack-131009089.html); [Yahoo teardown report](https://currently.att.yahoo.com/att/iphone-air-magsafe-battery-gets-121624437.html)
- Nearly 5 inches long. Its internal cell is 2.72 mm thick — [Yahoo teardown report](https://currently.att.yahoo.com/att/iphone-air-magsafe-battery-gets-121624437.html)
- It "absolutely does not fit properly on anything else" (it is designed only for the Air) — [AppleInsider, Sep 11 2025](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/09/11/magsafe-battery-for-iphone-air-absolutely-does-not-fit-properly-on-anything-else)
- Apple: up to 40 h video playback with the MagSafe Battery, vs 27 h without — [Apple Support tech specs](https://support.apple.com/en-us/125092)
- Tom's Guide: 17 h 15 min web surfing with the pack, vs 12 h 02 min without — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/iphone-air-battery-life-tested)

### Inferences
- The pack lets the buyer carry the extra weight only on heavy days, keeping the phone at 165 g day to day. Even with the pack, combined weight is very likely still below the Duo (exact weight unverified).
- Combined cost: Air 256 GB plus pack = $1,198, still under the $1,300 budget floor.

### Gaps
- **Weight of the iPhone Air MagSafe Battery: not found** in retrieved sources. It was not confirmed, so do not quote a figure.

## 7. Foldable specifics: price, weight vs iPhone 18 Pro Max, app support, durability, stock

### Takeaway
The Duo costs $1,999–$3,199 and weighs 254 g vs 249 g for the iPhone 18 Pro Max ($1,299+). Only about a third of top apps were optimized for its inner screen at launch. It is **constrained**: Apple US shows delivery around **January 22–29, 2027**, and Apple's COO said on Nov 2, 2026 that it's "hard to predict" when supply will improve.

### Cited Findings
**Weight and price vs iPhone 18 Pro Max**
- Duo 254 g (8.96 oz) vs **18 Pro Max 249 g (8.78 oz)**. The Pro Max uses an aluminum frame — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/weve-tested-the-iphone-duo-and-iphone-18-pro-max-heres-the-phone-you-should-actually-buy); [PhoneArena comparison](https://www.phonearena.com/phones/compare/Apple-iPhone-Duo,Apple-iPhone-18-Pro-Max/phones/12657,12880)
- iPhone 18 Pro Max from **$1,299**. iPhone 18 Pro from **$1,199** ($100 more than the 17 Pro) — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/weve-tested-the-iphone-duo-and-iphone-18-pro-max-heres-the-phone-you-should-actually-buy); [RefreshedApples](https://www.refreshedapples.com/blogs/news/apple-september-2026-event-resellers). Ghostek lists the 18 Pro Max at $1,499 for 512 GB, $1,899 for 1 TB and $2,499 for 2 TB (single source, unverified) — [Ghostek](https://ghostek.com/blogs/articles/apple-iphone-duo-vs-iphone-18-pro-max)

**Software and app support on the 7.6-inch inner screen**
